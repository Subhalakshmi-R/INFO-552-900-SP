Vet Clinics in Philadelphia

Finding a trusted veterinarian is one of the most important steps for new pet owners. Below are some general vet clinics in Philadelphia, along with tips for your first visit and affordable care options.

General Vet Clinics

Penn Vet - Ryan Veterinary Hospital

Located at the University of Pennsylvania, Ryan Veterinary Hospital is one of the top veterinary facilities in the country. Offers primary care, specialty services, and 24-hour emergency care.

vet.upenn.edu/ryan

Passyunk Animal Hospital

A neighborhood vet clinic in South Philadelphia offering wellness exams, vaccinations, dental care, and surgery. Known for its friendly staff and community focus.

ACCT Philly Wellness Services

Philadelphia's city shelter, ACCT Philly, occasionally offers low-cost wellness clinics and vaccination events. Check their website for upcoming dates and availability.

acctphilly.org

What to Expect at Your First Vet Visit

  • Bring any medical records from the shelter or breeder where you adopted your pet.
  • Write down any questions you have about food, behavior, or health concerns.
  • Your vet will do a physical exam and may recommend vaccinations or parasite prevention.
  • Ask about spaying or neutering if your pet has not already been fixed.
  • Set up a schedule for routine wellness visits - typically once a year for adult pets.

Tips for Affordable Veterinary Care

Low-Cost Options Look for low-cost vaccination clinics offered by local shelters and rescue organizations throughout the year. ACCT Philly and other nonprofit organizations sometimes host community wellness days with reduced-cost services.
Consider Pet Insurance Pet insurance can help cover unexpected veterinary costs. Research plans and compare coverage before your pet has a health issue, as most plans do not cover pre-existing conditions.

For Emergencies

If your pet has a medical emergency outside of regular business hours, visit the Emergency Contacts page for 24-hour emergency vet hospitals in Philadelphia.
