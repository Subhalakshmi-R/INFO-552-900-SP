Adoption Centers in Philadelphia

Thinking about adopting a pet? Philadelphia has several shelters and rescue organizations ready to help you find your perfect match.

Local Shelters and Rescue Groups

ACCT Philly

Philadelphia's official city shelter for stray and surrendered animals. ACCT Philly has a large number of dogs and cats available for adoption at any time. They also offer low-cost veterinary services.

acctphilly.org

Pennsylvania SPCA

One of the oldest animal welfare organizations in the country, with a location in Philadelphia. Offers adoption services, humane law enforcement, and community programs.

pspca.org

Morris Animal Refuge

The oldest animal shelter in the United States, located in Philadelphia. Focuses on adoption and community education. Cats and dogs are available regularly.

The Adoption Process - Basics

  • Visit the shelter's website to view available animals and submit an application online.
  • Most shelters require an adoption application, a brief interview, and an adoption fee.
  • Adopted pets are typically spayed or neutered, vaccinated, and microchipped before going home.
  • Bring all household members to meet the pet before adopting, including other pets if possible.
  • Ask shelter staff about the animal's history, temperament, and any special care needs.

Preparing for Your New Pet

Before You Bring Them Home Set up a quiet, safe space for your new pet before they arrive. Have food, water, a bed, and a litter box or waste bags ready. Schedule a vet appointment within the first week of adoption to establish care.
Give Them Time to Adjust New pets often need two to four weeks to settle in. Be patient and keep their routine consistent. Limit visitors during the first few days so your pet can adjust at their own pace.
