Pet Rules and Fines in Philadelphia

Understanding local rules helps you be a responsible pet owner and avoid fines. This page covers Philadelphia's key regulations for dogs and cats.

Leash Rules

In Philadelphia, dogs must be on a leash at all times when in public, including city parks, sidewalks, and shared spaces. The leash must be no longer than six feet.

Off-Leash Areas Off-leash areas are only permitted in designated dog parks or fenced areas within certain parks. Always look for posted signs before allowing your dog off-leash.

Waste Cleanup

Pet owners are required by city ordinance to pick up and properly dispose of their pet's waste in all public spaces, including sidewalks, parks, and common areas of apartment buildings.

Failure to clean up after your pet can result in a fine. Always carry waste bags with you on every walk.

Dog Licensing

All dogs in Philadelphia must be licensed. A dog license is required by law and helps ensure your dog can be identified and returned to you if lost.

License Type Details
Annual Dog License Required for all dogs over three months old living in Philadelphia
Spayed / Neutered Dog Lower fee for dogs that have been spayed or neutered
Renewal Licenses must be renewed annually

Noise and Nuisance Rules

Excessive barking or other noise disturbances from pets can be reported to animal control. Pet owners are responsible for managing their pet's behavior in shared spaces such as apartment buildings and neighborhoods.

Responsible Pet Ownership

  • Keep your dog's vaccinations current - especially rabies, which is required by law.
  • Make sure your pet wears an ID tag with your current contact information.
  • Microchipping your pet is strongly recommended and can be done at most vet clinics.
  • Do not leave pets unattended in cars, especially in warm weather.
  • Report stray or injured animals to ACCT Philly or animal control.
