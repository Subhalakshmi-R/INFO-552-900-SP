Welcome to Philly Pet Map

New to Philadelphia with a pet? Finding basic info like where dogs are allowed or what the leash rules are took me way longer than it should have. This site pulls it all into one place.

About This Site

Philly Pet Map is designed for new and first-time pet owners who recently adopted a pet or moved to Philadelphia with a dog or cat. Whether you need a park to walk your dog, a café that allows pets, or an emergency vet number at midnight - this site has you covered.

Use the navigation bar above to explore each section. All information links back to official Philadelphia city resources and trusted local organizations.

Featured Pet Tip

Did You Know? In Philadelphia, dogs must be on a leash no longer than six feet in all city parks. Waste bags are required-rangers do issue fines, and other dog owners will notice if you don't pick up.

What You Will Find Here

This site is organized into easy-to-use sections. Here is a quick overview of what each page covers:

  • Pet-Friendly Parks - Dog parks, walking areas, leash rules, and safety tips
  • Pet-Friendly Cafés - Outdoor seating spots that welcome pets
  • Pet Product Stores - Where to buy food, supplies, and grooming products
  • Vet Clinics - General and affordable veterinary care in Philadelphia
  • Adoption Centers - Local shelters and how to adopt responsibly
  • Pet Rules and Fines - Leash laws, licensing, cleanup rules, and fines
  • Emergency Contacts - 24-hour vets, animal control, and poison control
  • Resources - Official links, maps, and a suggestion form
