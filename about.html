About the Community

Who is Philly Pet Map for, and why does this resource exist? This page explains the community this site is designed to serve.

Who This Site Serves

Philly Pet Map is designed for new and first-time pet owners in Philadelphia. This includes:

  • People who recently adopted a dog or cat
  • New Philadelphia residents who moved to the city with a pet
  • Students and young professionals living in apartments
  • Families getting their first pet
  • Anyone who wants a simple guide to pet-friendly Philadelphia

Why This Resource Was Created

New pet owners often need quick access to local information - where to walk, where pets are welcome, where to buy supplies, how to find a vet, and what to do in an emergency. This information currently exists in many different places, including city websites, Google Maps, clinic websites, and social media.

Because the information is scattered, it can be confusing and time-consuming to find. Philly Pet Map brings the most important pet-related information into one organized place. The goal is not to replace official sources, but to make important information easier to find, understand, and use.

What This Community Needs

Based on a review of official Philadelphia city resources, park regulations, and local pet organization websites, new pet owners in Philadelphia commonly need:

  • Information about local dog parks and walking areas
  • A list of pet-friendly cafés and outdoor spaces
  • Nearby pet supply stores and grooming options
  • Affordable and trustworthy vet clinics
  • Guidance on adoption and responsible pet ownership
  • Clear explanations of city rules, leash laws, and licensing
  • Emergency contact numbers that are easy to find quickly

How This Site Helps

Philly Pet Map uses simple language, clear categories, and short descriptions so that users can find what they need without reading through long official documents. The site is also designed to be easy to use on a phone, since many people search for pet information on the go.

Our Approach Every page links back to official Philadelphia sources. If something has changed, the official link will have the latest version.
