Poison Control

ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center If you think your pet has eaten something toxic, call immediately:

(888) 426-4435
Available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
Note: A consultation fee may apply.
Common household items that are toxic to pets: Chocolate, grapes, raisins, onions, garlic, xylitol (found in sugar-free gum), certain houseplants, ibuprofen, and acetaminophen. Keep these out of reach at all times.

Lost Pet Resources

  • Report a lost pet to ACCT Philly immediately.
  • Post on Nextdoor, neighborhood Facebook groups, and apps like PawBoost or Lost My Doggie.
  • Contact local vet clinics and shelters with a photo and description of your pet.
  • Make sure your pet is microchipped and wearing an up-to-date ID tag at all times.
